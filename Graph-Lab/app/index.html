Lab Exam Simulation
A mock lab exam on the Required topics. Every attempt uses freshly generated graphs.

      

        
Nine questions, twenty minutes

        
Six hand traces in the course document's style (BFS, DFS, adjacency matrix, BFS distance, Dijkstra, Kruskal) and three questions where you name the right algorithm for a story. Work on paper first, as you would in the lab. Neighbours are always taken in alphabetical order.
